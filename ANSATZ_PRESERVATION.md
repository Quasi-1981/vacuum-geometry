# Ansatz preservation — a request, not a licence term

> **This is the author's request and a community norm, NOT a condition of the licence.**
> The text of this project is under plain [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE). CC BY 4.0 forbids
> imposing additional restrictions, so nothing on this page limits the rights that
> licence grants you. You may ignore everything below and remain fully within your
> rights. The ask is made because the project believes the request is reasonable —
> not because it can be enforced.

If you adapt, extend or substantially reuse this work, the author asks that you
preserve three foundations. They are what the work *is*; a derivative that drops
them is a different project wearing the same words.

## 1. Field-first ontology

The energy field is the sole actor. Matter, time and physical constants are
emergent outputs of the field — not independent inputs or separate postulates.
The request: do not silently revert to treating particles or solitons as primary
objects while still citing this work as the basis.

## 2. Handle minimality

The prime directive is not to multiply free parameters. The target is a single
structural handle. The request: if a derivative adds a free parameter, say so
explicitly, and state which of the two it is — an output of the field, or a
provisionally necessary assumption still awaiting derivation.

## 3. Dynamics

Statics produces nothing. The request: any mechanism claiming to derive mass,
time or a particle from a purely static configuration should be flagged as
contradicting this ansatz, rather than presented as a continuation of it.

---

## Why this is a separate file

Earlier versions of the project's licence carried these three points as an
additional condition attached to CC BY. That construction is self-contradictory:
CC BY 4.0 explicitly prohibits further restrictions, so "CC BY plus a condition"
is no longer CC BY — either the badge misleads, or the condition is void. Rather
than leave the ambiguity for a reader to resolve, the licence was made plain and
the ask was moved here, where its status is stated outright.

Attribution remains a genuine licence requirement and is described in
[LICENSE](LICENSE).
