# Vacuum Geometry

One deductive chain of pure mathematics: four declared premises, **one of them dimensional**
(Λ) → the symmetric group S(d+1) → the terminal step of the derived series → the
root lattice A_d → the column and its compact dual → sign and signature.

**Site:** https://quasi-1981.github.io/vacuum-geometry/

Every link carries the address of its primary act. The exposition is a representation of
what is already proved, not a new derivation.

## Boundaries of this publication

1. **Statuses are not uniform.** Some links are measured (probe + independent
   verification), others are candidates — an inductive form fitted to data, neither a
   derivation nor a test. The grade is written at each link; the word "theorem" does not
   raise it.
2. **This is a fragment, not a complete theory.** What is published is the prime floor —
   the pure mathematics of the chain. The bridge and interpretive layers are not part of
   this publication.
3. **The classics carry multiplicity zero.** Root lattices, Weyl chambers, Pontryagin
   duality, Gauss sums, Schur's lemma, the Levi decomposition are reference, not evidence
   in favour of anything here.
