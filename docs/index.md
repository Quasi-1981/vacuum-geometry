# Prime-floor derivation chain

**One deductive chain of pure mathematics**: four declared premises, **one of them dimensional**
($\Lambda$) → the symmetric group $S_{d+1}$ → the terminal step of the derived series → the root lattice
$A_d$ → the column and its compact dual → sign and signature.

Every link carries the **address of its primary act** (probe · independent verification · seal).
The exposition is a **representation of what is already proved**, not a new derivation: no statement
changes its status here.

## Three honest boundaries of this publication

1. **The statuses are not uniform.** Some links are `⚓ measured` (probe + independent verification),
   others are `candidate/cast` (an inductive form fitted to data — **neither a derivation nor a test**).
   The grade of each link is written at the link. The word "theorem" does not raise the grade.
2. **This is a fragment, not a complete theory.** What is published is the prime floor — the pure
   mathematics of the chain. The bridge and interpretive layers are **not** part of this publication.
3. **The classics carry multiplicity zero.** Root lattices $A_d$, Weyl chambers, Pontryagin duality,
   Gauss sums, Schur's lemma, the Levi decomposition are **reference**, not evidence in our favour.

## The rule for writing numbers

A number counts as a legitimate piece of writing only if it is a **citizen of a solvable tower over
$\mathbb{Q}$**. A quantity whose minimal tower has an unsolvable factor is treated as a **writing
artefact**, not as an object of the theory. The rule is machine-enforced: every published number
carries the obligatory fields {value · address · unit · type · algebraic form}, and the site is not
built until the check has passed.

*Boundary of the rule: it measures **algebraic** numbers (minimal polynomial over $\mathbb{Q}$).
$\pi$ and the transcendentals lie outside its action and are marked separately.*
